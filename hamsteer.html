Hamsteer

Part of my Experimental Games Module

Hamsteer was a party game with the experimental feature of using leap motion hand trackers for player input. This game worked off of a 120 second timer that would change games every 20 seconds and those games were collect the coins, king of the hill and a race minigame. These minigames were then all repeated once and then at the end a leaderboard was shown and a player was deemed the winner! This was a group project in which i had to work with fellow artists, designers and programmers and this gave me a lot of insight into how working in teams within industry can often feel like as merging assets with all three specalties came with challenges and problems that i could never have predicted for example designers and artists often import assets with different than to what unity uses but also ensuring that desingers assets can be used and work in unision with scripts and art assets was a big challenge and often lead to a lot of back and forth where i'd really have to get down into fine detail for what i needed to ensure that we both understood what needed to be made.
