Signal Lost Game

Part of my Professional Awareness Module

Signal lost was a group project i did with a classmate (a designer) and the idea behind the game was to create a story driven experience about someone working in a remote signal tower who wakes up to discover everyone is missing and that they have no memory of where they are or how they got there. Unfortunatly, due to the short timeframe nature of game jams, we only managed to get the start of the game complete, but we did leave the game on a cliffhanger for possible future expansion...
